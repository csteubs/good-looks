# Plans

Design documents written before larger features were built. They are kept as a
record of intent — what was scoped, what was deliberately left out, and why the
work was phased the way it was.

**These are historical, not instructions.** They describe the state of the code
at the time they were written, and some of what they propose was changed or
dropped during implementation. For what the code does *now*, read
[../ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md); for what actually landed and why, read
[../DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md).

Two things in them are out of date by design:

- They refer to `PROJECT-CONTEXT.md` and `.glaze_memory/`. That content now lives
  in [../ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) and [../DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md).
- Their closing "update PROJECT-CONTEXT.md after completion" steps are superseded
  by the docs checklist in the pull request template.
